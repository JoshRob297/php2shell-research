chain D / EG(objects_store) at shutdown — measurement of 2026-10-24
===================================================================

Question: is the object store left stale or dangling when a request dies in the middle of
its startup, so that the next request on the same worker can consume it?

Target
------
php:8.4.26-apache (mod_php, prefork), memory_limit 128M, post_max_size 8M
Worker pid 17, same worker for all three requests
Breakpoint: php_request_shutdown, main/main.c:1891

Sequence on that worker
-----------------------
1. warmup GET                 -> HTTP 200
2. chunked 70M at startup     -> HTTP 500, "Allowed memory size of 134217728 bytes exhausted
                                 (tried to allocate 102400 bytes) in Unknown on line 0"
3. GET after the OOM          -> HTTP 200   (the worker survived)

At the shutdown breakpoint, three observations, one per request:

SHUTDOWN ob=0x7f1b30451000 top=1 size=1024 flh=-1 err=30719
0x7f1b30451000: 0x0000000000000000  0x0000000000000000
SHUTDOWN ob=0x7f1b30451000 top=1 size=1024 flh=-1 err=30719
0x7f1b30451000: 0x0000000000000000  0x0000000000000000
SHUTDOWN ob=0x7f1b30451000 top=1 size=1024 flh=-1 err=30719
0x7f1b30451000: 0x0000000000000000  0x0000000000000000

Second run, same shape, with the VM stack included:

SHUTDOWN ob=0x7f0ed8651000 top=1 size=1024 flh=-1 err=30719 ced=(nil) vs=0x7f0ed8611000 vst=0x7f0ed8611020 vse=0x7f0ed8651000   (x3)

Reading
-------
- The store pointer (`ob`), its next-free slot (`top=1`), its capacity (`size=1024`) and the
  free-list head (`flh=-1`) are identical in all three shutdowns.
- `top=1` with handle 0 reserved means the store holds no live objects at shutdown, and the
  first two bucket entries read as zero. That is consistent across the healthy request, the
  one that died at startup, and the one that followed.
- `current_execute_data`, `vm_stack` top and end are also identical.
- No crash, no dangling pointer, and the worker kept serving.

What this measures, and what it does not
----------------------------------------
Measured: the state of the object store header at shutdown is the same whether or not the
request died in the middle of startup. The "stale EG(objects_store)" framing, where the
pointer or its bookkeeping is left behind for the next request, is not supported by this.

Not measured: the contents of the entire 1024-entry bucket array, and the case where the
request reaches shutdown with live objects. Other work covers that variant and reaches further
than this measurement does; this file does not contradict it.
